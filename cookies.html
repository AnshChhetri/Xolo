Cookie Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

✓ XOLO does not use cookies, and does not show a cookie banner, because none are needed.

1. We don't use cookies

XOLO sets no HTTP cookies at all — no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies, and no third-party tracking cookies. There is no cookie-consent banner on the site because there is nothing that legally requires your consent to store.

2. What we do use instead: local storage

XOLO saves two small pieces of information directly in your browser's local storage (a different, non-cookie technology that never leaves your device or gets sent to us):

Stored itemPurposeShared with us?
Theme preferenceRemembers whether you last used light or dark modeNo — stays on your device
Last room codeConvenience, so you don't have to retype a room code you were just inNo — stays on your device

You can clear this at any time through your browser's settings (usually under "Clear browsing data" / "Site data"), or by using a private/incognito window.

3. Third-party resources

To run, the XOLO page loads a few code and font libraries directly from third-party servers. As a normal technical side-effect of your browser requesting these files, the provider sees your IP address — the same way any website works. We don't control or receive this data ourselves:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com)
  • Cloudflare CDN (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) — for React, React DOM, and Babel
  • Tailwind CSS CDN (cdn.tailwindcss.com)
  • Firebase / Google (gstatic.com, firebaseio.com) — for the live multiplayer database and anonymous sign-in

These providers may set their own cookies or use their own storage under their own policies when you load their files — this is outside XOLO's control. See Google's Cookie Policy for the Google-operated services above (Fonts, Firebase).

4. Changes to this policy

If XOLO ever adds analytics, advertising, or anything that does require cookies or consent, this page — and a proper consent mechanism — will be updated before that happens.
