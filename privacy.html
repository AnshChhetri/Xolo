Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information XOLO ("we," "the game," "the site") collects when you use it, why, and what rights you have under Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

XOLO is a free, fan-made football auction and league simulation game. It is run by an individual, not a registered company.

1. Who runs XOLO

Ansh Chhetri
Contact: ansh.chhetri2007@gmail.com

2. What information we collect

  • A display name you choose. When you join or create a room, you type in a name shown to other players. You do not need to use your real name — a nickname works fine, and we recommend using one.
  • Gameplay data. The players you bid on, the squad you build, and actions within a room (bids, votes, chat-style logs the game generates) are stored so the game can sync in real time between everyone in that room.
  • An anonymous device identifier. We use Firebase's anonymous authentication to give your browser session a random ID. This ID is not linked to your real identity, email, or any account — there is no sign-up or login.
  • Local device storage (not a cookie). Your browser saves your light/dark theme preference and the last room code you used, purely so the app remembers your preference. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. See our Cookie Policy for details.
  • Standard technical data seen by our infrastructure providers. Like any website, when your browser loads XOLO it necessarily reveals your IP address and browser type to the servers delivering the page (see Section 4). We do not separately collect, log, or analyze this ourselves — we have no analytics or tracking tools installed on XOLO.

3. What we do NOT collect

We do not ask for or store your email address, phone number, physical address, date of birth, government ID, or any payment information. XOLO has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking scripts of any kind.

4. Third-party services we rely on

XOLO is a single web page hosted for free and relies on a few external services to run. Loading these is a normal technical requirement of the page working, and each provider may see your IP address as part of that:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — provides the real-time database and anonymous sign-in that let multiple players share a room live. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) — hosts this website. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Cloudflare CDN — delivers the React, React DOM, and Babel code libraries the page needs to run.
  • Tailwind CSS CDN — delivers the styling library.
  • Google Fonts — delivers the Inter and Teko fonts used on the site.

None of these providers are paid or instructed by us to track you, build an advertising profile, or sell your data — we don't use any of them for that purpose, and we don't have any advertising partners at all.

5. Cross-border data transfer

Because Firebase and Google's infrastructure may process and store data on servers outside India, your information may be transferred internationally. We rely on these providers' own security and privacy safeguards for this. In line with the DPDP Act, we disclose this transfer here rather than hide it.

6. Children's data

XOLO does not currently have an age restriction and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children beyond the same optional display name any player can choose, and we run no advertising or behavioural tracking of any kind — for anyone, of any age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has shared personal information with us that you'd like removed, contact us at the email above and we will act on it.

7. How long we keep data

Room and game data live in our Firebase database for the duration of a game session and may be periodically cleared. Anonymous identifiers are not designed to be linked back to a real-world identity.

8. Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

As a person whose data we process ("Data Principal"), you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data (if any) we hold that's linked to you
  • Ask us to correct or update it
  • Ask us to erase it
  • Withdraw any consent you've given, at any time
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if unresolved, escalate it to the Data Protection Board of India
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity

To exercise any of these rights, email ansh.chhetri2007@gmail.com. Since XOLO does not collect identifying information (no accounts, no email sign-up), most requests will simply confirm we don't hold data traceable to you beyond an anonymous session ID.

9. Security

We rely on Firebase's built-in security infrastructure and access rules to protect data in transit and at rest. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we don't collect sensitive data that would make a breach especially harmful.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest revision.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email ansh.chhetri2007@gmail.com.
