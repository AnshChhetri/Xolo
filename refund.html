Refund Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

✓ XOLO is completely free. No payments are collected, so there is nothing to refund.

1. No payments, no purchases

XOLO does not charge for access, and there are currently no subscriptions, in-app purchases, donations, or any other paid features anywhere on the platform. Every part of the game — creating rooms, joining auctions, bidding, and simulating the league — is free to use.

2. In-game currency has no real value

The fictional "₹Cr" budget used during the auction exists only within the game. It cannot be bought, sold, withdrawn, or exchanged for real money, and has no connection to any real payment.

3. If this changes in the future

If XOLO ever introduces a paid feature, this Refund Policy will be updated in advance to clearly explain what is refundable, the process for requesting a refund, and any applicable time limits — before you'd be asked to pay for anything.
